## Website

### Dedicated Legacy Giving Page
**Suggested URL:** midnightmission.org/legacy (or /golden-heart-legacy)

**Page Sections:**
1. **Hero** — Positioning statement + one compelling image
2. **Why Legacy Giving** — What a planned gift means for TMM's future
3. **Ways to Give** — Simple list: bequest, beneficiary designation, etc. (no jargon)
4. **Society Recognition** — What donors receive; invite to join
5. **FAQ** — Common questions answered simply
6. **Take Action** — Three CTAs: (1) Notify us of your gift, (2) FreeWill / online pledge tool, (3) Contact Tiffany

**Tone:** Warm, human, mission-driven. Avoid legal/financial language on the front end.

**Integration notes:**
- Link from: Ways to Give page, Annual Report page, Donation confirmation page, Gala landing page
- Add subtle callout to donation forms: *"Have you included TMM in your estate plans?"*

## Prospect Strategy (Tiffany — Extra Credit)

### Target Groups by Priority

#### Tier 1 — Highest Potential
| Group | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Long-tenured donors (10+ years) | Deep connection; trust already built |
| Gala attendees — repeat | Emotional engagement; Golden Heart brand familiarity |
| Mid-level donors ($1K–$9,999/year) | Capacity exists; legacy may be their biggest gift |
| Board alumni | Institutional loyalty; often still engaged post-service |

#### Tier 2 — Strong Potential
| Group | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Event attendees without major gift history | Mission-adjacent; need a lower-barrier way to give big |
| Volunteers, especially long-term | Deep personal connection to the work |
| Program alumni and families | Personal story of transformation; want to give back |
| Professionals in estate/financial planning | Peer-to-peer credibility; may be clients of prospects |

#### Tier 3 — Cultivation Needed
| Group | Why |
|-------|-----|
| New major donors (under 3 years) | Deepen relationship before introducing legacy |
| Newsletter subscribers with no gift history | Interest established; need engagement before ask |
| Social media followers with high engagement | Passive supporters who may be ready to step forward |

### Prospect Qualification Questions (for Tiffany's conversations)
- How long have you been connected to The Midnight Mission?
- Is there a program or population you feel most passionate about?
- Have you ever thought about how you want your giving to extend beyond your lifetime?
- Are you working with a financial advisor or estate attorney on your planning?

## Newsletter Strategy (Email + Mail)

### Frequency
| Channel | Frequency | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Email newsletter | 1 legacy-focused feature per quarter | Can be a sidebar/section, not a standalone email |
| Print newsletter | 1 legacy story per issue | Donor spotlight or impact story works well |
| Standalone legacy email | 1–2 per year | Around year-end and/or estate planning awareness month (October) |

### Content Ideas
- **Donor Spotlight:** "Why I chose to include The Midnight Mission in my will" (short, first-person)
- **Impact Forward:** "Here's what your legacy gift will help make possible in 10 years"
- **Myth-Busting:** "You don't have to be wealthy to leave a legacy gift"
- **How-To:** "3 simple ways to include The Midnight Mission in your estate plans"
- **Society Introduction:** Annual spotlight on the Golden Heart Legacy Society

### Print Mail Considerations
- Legacy messaging resonates especially well with older donors — don't abandon print
- Consider a dedicated legacy giving insert in the annual appeal mailing
- BRE (business reply envelope) not needed — direct to Tiffany or website

## FreeWill & Similar Services

### Recommended Integration: FreeWill
- **What it is:** Free online will-writing tool; nonprofits can offer it to donors at no cost
- **Why it matters:** Removes the #1 barrier (cost/complexity of an estate attorney for simple wills)
- **How to integrate:** Link from legacy giving web page; include in legacy email CTAs
- **Talking point:** "We can help you take the first step — for free — through our partner FreeWill"

### Other Options to Evaluate
| Service | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| Giving Docs | Similar to FreeWill; good for beneficiary designations |
| Tomorrow | Mobile-first will creation; good for younger donors |
| Trust & Will | More robust; may appeal to mid-level donors |

**Recommendation:** Start with FreeWill (most nonprofit-friendly, widely used) and evaluate based on usage.
