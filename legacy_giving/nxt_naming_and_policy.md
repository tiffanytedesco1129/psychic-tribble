## Part 2: Internal Legacy Giving Policy

### Purpose
This policy establishes the internal procedures for identifying, cultivating, acknowledging, and stewarding donors who include The Midnight Mission (TMM) in their estate plans. The goal is to ensure consistency, privacy, and meaningful donor relationships.

### Definition of a Legacy Gift
A legacy gift (also called a planned gift) is any charitable contribution arranged during a donor's lifetime but typically received after death. Types include:

- Bequest in a will or living trust
- Beneficiary designation (life insurance, retirement account, bank/brokerage account)
- Charitable Remainder Trust (CRT)
- Charitable Lead Trust (CLT)
- Real property gift
- Life insurance policy assignment

### Minimum Threshold for Society Membership
There is no minimum gift size required for Golden Heart Legacy Society membership. Membership is based on confirmed charitable intent, not gift amount.

### Confidentiality
All legacy gift information is strictly confidential. Information is shared internally only on a need-to-know basis (Relationship Manager, Development Director, CEO). No donor's intent is shared publicly without explicit written consent.

### Gift Acceptance
TMM will not accept gifts that:
- Conflict with the organization's mission or values
- Carry conditions that limit TMM's ability to use the gift for its charitable purpose
- Present undue administrative burden or legal risk

Gifts with restrictions will be reviewed by the Development Director and CEO, with legal counsel when appropriate.

### Acknowledgment Protocol
| Timing | Action | Owner |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Within 48 hours of confirmation | Thank-you call | Tiffany |
| Within 2 weeks | Formal welcome letter (from David) | Val drafts, David signs |
| Within 30 days | Welcome into Society (recognition pref. confirmed) | Tiffany |
| Annually | Personal touchpoint + impact update | Tiffany |

### Record Keeping
All legacy gift information must be entered into Raiser's Edge NXT within 5 business days of learning of the intent. Physical copies of signed pledge documents must be stored in the locked Development filing cabinet under "Legacy Giving — [Last Name]."

### Revocation
Donors may revoke or change their legacy gift at any time. TMM staff will thank the donor for their previous consideration and keep the relationship warm. Records will be updated accordingly in NXT.

### Annual Review
The Development team will review the Legacy Society membership list annually to:
- Confirm all records are current
- Ensure stewardship touchpoints are scheduled
- Update estimated pipeline values for organizational planning
