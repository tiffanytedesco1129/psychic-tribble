Upload Donor / Volunteer Data

Import a CSV or Excel export from Raiser's Edge or Get Connected. The app will categorize companies and flag new sponsorship targets automatically.

Raiser's Edge

Export a Gift report or Organization constituent list that includes gift type (Cash / Gift in Kind), company name, and optionally contact info and address.

Typical columns in a RE export:

  • Organization Name / Company
  • Gift Type (critical — detects Gift in Kind)
  • Gift Amount, Gift Date
  • Contact Name, Title, Email, Phone
  • Address Line 1, City, State, ZIP
Get Connected

Export a Volunteer or Sign-up report that includes the volunteer's employer/organization. The app uses this to find companies already connected through their employees but never asked for cash sponsorship.

Typical columns in a GC export:

  • Organization / Company / Employer
  • First Name, Last Name (or Full Name)
  • Email, Phone
  • Opportunity / Event Name
  • Shift Date, Hours
